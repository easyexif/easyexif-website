Your photos stay yours

The EasyEXIF tools run entirely in your browser. When you choose a photo, your browser reads it with JavaScript on this page. The file is not sent to a server, and we do not store, log or look at it.

What leaves your device

Nothing about your photos. The only network requests this site makes are for its own pages, scripts and fonts. If you click “Open in OpenStreetMap” on a photo’s location, that link opens in a new tab and the coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap, but only because you clicked it.

Check it yourself

Open your browser’s network panel while using a tool, or disconnect from the internet after the page loads. Everything still works.

Edited files

Edited, cleaned or copied photos are created inside your browser and saved straight to your downloads folder. Your originals are never changed.

The EasyEXIF extension

The Chrome extension also processes images locally. Its own privacy notes are on its store listing.
