What you can change

Edit the date taken, camera make and model, lens, software, artist, copyright, description, comment, exposure settings, orientation and GPS location. Anything you leave alone is preserved byte-for-byte. Clear a field to delete that tag.

Editing works on JPEG, PNG and WebP files. When a photo is edited, the proprietary maker note and the small embedded thumbnail are not carried across, because their internal addresses cannot be safely moved. The image itself is never re-compressed.

Questions

Does editing upload my photo?

No. The editor is designed to work locally in your browser so you can inspect or change metadata without sending images to a server.

Will it change the original file?

No. Your browser can’t modify files in place. You download a new copy and keep the original.

Is it okay to fake a date or location?

Use it for fixing mistakes: scanned photos, wrong camera clocks, photos taken with location services off. Please do not use it to mislead people.
