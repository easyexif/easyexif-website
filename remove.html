Remove EXIF data and location from photos

Strip GPS coordinates, camera serial numbers, timestamps and other hidden details before you post or send a photo. Drop in as many as you like.

How to remove EXIF data

  1. Drop in your photosJPEG, PNG and WebP are cleaned without touching the pixels. Other formats are re-drawn as a clean PNG.
  2. Check what was foundEach photo shows what it was carrying, such as GPS, serial numbers or editing history.
  3. DownloadSave one photo, or all of them as a ZIP. Each is re-read afterwards to confirm it is clean.

What gets removed

EXIF (camera, settings, dates, GPS and the embedded thumbnail), XMP, IPTC captions and credits, JPEG comments, PNG text chunks, and any extra data appended after the image. The picture itself is not re-compressed.

Questions

Does removing EXIF reduce quality?

No. Photos are not re-encoded. Only the metadata around the image is removed, so the pixels stay exactly the same.

Do social networks already remove it?

Many do, but not all, and not for every kind of upload (original-quality downloads, email attachments, forum and marketplace listings, direct messages). Cleaning a photo yourself means you do not have to guess.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Cleaning happens inside this tab, and the files never touch a server.
